About the studio

Small on purpose.

Northbound started in 2020 as two engineers tired of watching good projects drown in process. Six years later we're still six people, still turning down more work than we take, and still writing the code ourselves.

How we work

A few things we hold to on every project

None of this is groundbreaking. It's just easy to lose once a studio gets big enough to need a process for having a process.

  • 01

    You talk to the person building it

    No account managers relaying messages. The engineer on your project is in your inbox and your calls.

  • 02

    Fewer projects, more attention

    We cap concurrent projects deliberately. It means longer waitlists sometimes, but nobody's work gets the leftover hours.

  • 03

    Boring technology, on purpose

    We reach for the well-understood tool over the exciting one. Your product should still be maintainable in three years, by someone who isn't us.

  • 04

    We say no when we should

    If a request doesn't serve the actual goal, we'll say so before we build it — even if that's not what you wanted to hear that week.

A short history

Six years, roughly

  • 2020

    Two founders, one client

    Started with a single fintech client and a shared laptop charger. That first project is still running in production, mostly unchanged.

  • 2022

    Grew to six, stopped there

    Brought on four more engineers and designers, then deliberately capped headcount so the studio wouldn't outgrow the thing that made it work.

  • 2026

    41 projects in, still picky

    Still turning down more work than we take on, and still shipping every project ourselves rather than handing it off.
